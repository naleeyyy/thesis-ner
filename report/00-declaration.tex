\section*{Statutory Declaration}

I hereby declare under oath that the submitted Practical Work report titled
``LLM-Assisted Annotation and Evaluation for Albanian Named Entity Recognition'' has been
carried out by me at the Institute of Machine Learning, Johannes Kepler University Linz.
The information derived from the literature has been duly acknowledged in the text and a
list of references provided. This report has not previously been submitted, in whole or in
part, to any other degree or diploma at this or any other institution.

\paragraph{Statement on the use of artificial intelligence.}
Large language models were used in this work in two distinct roles, both of which are
disclosed here.

As part of the method under study, Claude Sonnet~5 produced the pre-annotations whose
effect on human annotators is measured in Section~\ref{sec:results:annotation}. Claude
Haiku~4.5 was evaluated as an alternative during a pilot and was not used for any corpus
labels.

As a development and writing assistant, Claude Opus~5 and Claude Fable~5.1, accessed
through Claude Code, were used under my direction to implement and test the data
processing, training and evaluation code, to run experiments and compute the reported
statistics, to check bibliographic references against publisher records, and to draft
report text that I subsequently revised and rewrote. The research question, the
experimental design, the annotation protocol and the annotation campaign, the
interpretation of the results and the final text are my own, and I take full
responsibility for the content of this report.
